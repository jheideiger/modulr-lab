→ history

a history
of the signal.

from the Telharmonium to Eurorack: how sound synthesis got here.

→ why

understanding where we came from.

Modular wasn't born with Eurorack, and it hasn't always existed in this form either. It's the outcome — a provisional one — of a century and a half of attempts to tame electricity in the service of sound.

These five stages retrace the major milestones of that history: from multi-ton machines of the early twentieth century to the small format that now sits on your desk.
