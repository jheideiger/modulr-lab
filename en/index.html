manifesto

Modular synthesis isn't just a technique — it's a way of thinking about sound. Where a classic synthesizer offers a fixed path from oscillator to output, the modular opens up a field where every cable you plug in redraws the signal's route.

modulr lab was born from a simple wish: to share this practice with anyone who's heard of it but never quite dared to jump in. No displays of virtuosity, no gratuitous jargon — just the basics, laid out clearly, so you can decide for yourself whether this world is for you.
