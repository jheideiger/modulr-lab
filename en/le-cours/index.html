→ the course

modular
starter kit.

four lessons to understand eurorack, from signal to the principle that connects every module.

→ philosophy

learn by understanding, not by copying.

This course isn't a list of recipes. It won't tell you "plug this here, turn that knob there." It explains why things work the way they do — so that every patch you build afterward is truly yours, not a copy.

Each lesson takes 12 to 20 minutes to read, alongside a video. No technical prerequisites. Just curiosity.

→ who it's for

for the curious who never quite dared.

You make music, or you don't. You know your way around a DAW, or you don't. You've seen modular videos online and thought "this is fascinating but I don't understand any of it." This course is for you.

By the end of the four lessons, you'll know what a signal is, how the timbre of a sound is born, what the fundamental modules are for, and how a control voltage ties it all together to bring a patch to life.

A vibration, a signal, and the two quantities that describe it: frequency, which gives pitch, and amplitude, which gives level.
