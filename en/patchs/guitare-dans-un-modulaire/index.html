Plugging a guitar into a modular synth

One sustained chord, sent into a Eurorack system, and four patches that transform it. It all starts in the same place: the level. The rest fits into four notes, one per patch in the video.

video
the level

what they share: the level.

An audio signal in a modular system is 10 V peak to peak. An electric guitar puts out a few hundred millivolts. Plugged in directly, it produces almost nothing, and most people give up right there, thinking it doesn't work.

So the level has to be brought up before it enters the system. On the Shared System Plus, the CV Bus 1/4″ jack input does it: it has the gain needed, and its LEDs show the level reached. Turn the gain up until they show the right output level, without lighting the limiter. Without that input, a pedal, a preamp or an audio interface does the same job.

The signal is then picked up from another jack on the same color channel of the CV Bus. That's cable 1 and cable 2 in all four notes.
