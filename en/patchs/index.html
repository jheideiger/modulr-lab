→ how it works

The video shows the patch; the note lets you rebuild it. Each one follows the same structure: the gear used, with a generic equivalent for every module in case you don't have the same system, the diagram with numbered cables, step-by-step wiring and starting settings.

Then comes the part that matters most: what to listen for, three variations, and an exercise to do on your own, on your own system. The course lays down the concepts; the patches put them into practice.
