patch note 6 · MATHS

Make Noise MATHS: one slope, three inputs.

At the heart of MATHS there is a slope: a voltage that rises and falls back, over a time you set with Rise and Fall. You feed it in three ways: with a pulse, with a voltage, or with itself. This patch lets you hear all three, on the same melody from René.

video
result

what you’ll get.

A short melody from René played by the DPO. With a pulse, each note opens and closes: an envelope. With a voltage, it glides into the next: a portamento. With itself, the slope runs on its own and makes the sound breathe: an LFO.

level beginner to intermediate

time 30 min

modules MATHS · DPO · Optomix · René · Tempi · CV Bus

listening

what to listen for.

  1. A pulse: Trigger. Unplug cable 4 and patch René straight into the VCO: only channel 4 is left. Each gate from René fires the slope, which rises, falls back and opens the Optomix: that’s an envelope. Then slowly raise Rise: once the rise lasts longer than the gap between two steps, the sound only opens every other note. MATHS ignores the pulses that arrive while it is rising.
  2. A voltage: Signal. Plug cables 4 and 5 back in. The note voltage enters channel 1, which follows it with a lag: the notes glide from one to the next. Turn Rise and Fall on channel 1: Rise sets the glide up to a higher note, Fall the glide down to a lower one. MATHS can only slow a voltage down, never speed it up.
  3. Itself: Cycle. Unplug cable 2 and press Cycle on channel 4: at the end of each fall, the slope starts again on its own. That’s an LFO, opening and closing the sound without René. Rise equal to Fall, the sound breathes; short Rise and long Fall, it pulses.
variations

three variations.

  1. The shape of the slope. Lengthen Fall on channel 4, then turn Vari-Response from one end to the other. To the left, the note swells and stretches; to the right, it snaps and drops at once. The time hasn’t changed: the curve has.
  2. The 281. Cable 2 unplugged. On MATHS, channel 1 EOR to channel 4 Trigger, channel 4 EOC to channel 1 Trigger: the two slopes hand over to each other endlessly. Send channel 1 Unity to a second Optomix channel, on VCO B, to hear each slope on its own sound.
  3. The bouncing ball. The patch from the MATHS manual: channel 1 EOR to channel 4 Cycle In; channel 1 variable output to channel 4 Fall CV; channel 4 Unity to the Optomix Control; a pulse into channel 1 Trigger. Channel 1: Rise fully counter-clockwise, Fall at 3 o’clock, attenuverter around 2 o’clock. Channel 4: Rise fully counter-clockwise, Fall around noon. Each bounce is shorter than the last, until it stops.
your turn

your turn to listen.

MATHS as a clock divider. On channel 4, raise Rise until the sound only opens every other note. Write down the knob position. Keep going to every third note, then every fourth. Change Tempi’s tempo: do the positions move? Why?
